Scoring bands

Ordinal bands exist for UI clarity. They are not legal findings. Only four labels are allowed.

On this page

  1. Vocabulary
  2. Frozen cut points
  3. Illustrative reading
  4. UI requirements

Vocabulary

BandMeaning in product language
lowScore below elevated threshold with enough usable plies and narrow enough uncertainty
elevatedMid band; warrants careful human reading of features and notes
highScore at or above high threshold under the same sample gates
inconclusiveUncertainty too wide or usable sample too small

Never emit a definitive guilt label. Never invent a fifth band name in first-party UI.

Frozen cut points

From PROJECT_SPEC and calibration-v1. Let S be the score in [0, 1] and H the uncertainty half-width.

  1. If H > 0.20 OR usablePlies < 20 => inconclusive
  2. Else if S < 0.45 => low
  3. Else if S < 0.70 => elevated
  4. Else => high

Do not move these cut points to make acceptance fixtures pass. Fix code or mark a fixture out of scope with reason. A new calibration policy id is required for intentional prior changes.

Illustrative reading

  • A short amateur game with few usable plies often returns inconclusive even if match rates look unusual.
  • A long game with clocks and moderate match rates may land low or elevated depending on S.
  • Missing clocks increase half-width relative to an otherwise similar clocked game.

Acceptance fixtures A1-A5 are documented in analyzer acceptance notes; this wiki does not invent new numeric outcomes.

UI requirements

  • Show band, score, uncertainty, and methodology notes together.
  • Board replay and charts are optional companions on the report page; they must not replace uncertainty copy.
  • Landing marketing may show a stylized report sample; it must not claim live detection.
