Limits and non-goals

AxiomChess is intentionally narrow. This page lists what the product will not do in v1, so operators and users do not expect tribunal-grade findings.

On this page

  1. Non-goals
  2. Sample-size and data limits
  3. Legal and federation posture
  4. Claims this wiki will not make

Non-goals

  • Live cheat detection during ongoing games.
  • Account takeover, credential stuffing, captcha bypass, or any private-game access.
  • Scraping HTML behind login, circumventing rate limits, or violating site Terms of Service.
  • Definitive guilt labels without uncertainty and methodology.
  • Closed-source engine forks or undistributable nets.
  • Social features as a primary product (comments, reputation scores, public shaming feeds).
  • Guaranteeing legal findings for federations or platforms.

Sample-size and data limits

Reports become inconclusive when uncertainty half-width is greater than 0.20 or usable plies after book exclusion are fewer than 20. Missing clocks widen uncertainty relative to a clocked peer of similar length. Book-heavy openings reduce usable sample size by design.

Legal and federation posture

AxiomChess is an educational analysis aid. It does not replace human investigation, site fair-play teams, or arbitration processes. Prefer analyzing a player's own games or clearly public games.

Claims this wiki will not make

  • No guaranteed detection rates.
  • No invented uptime percentages.
  • No fabricated latency SLAs on these pages.
  • No third-party license text invented without upstream verification.
